## New genuine BRTI live reference lane
Official Kalshi WS endpoint: `wss://external-api-ws.kalshi.com/trade-api/ws/v2`. Authentication: standard signed GET handshake with the established server-side secrets. Subscriptions: `cfbenchmarks_value` (1 Hz, includes provider trailing 60-second and quarter-hour averaging metadata) and `cfbenchmarks_value_5hz` (up to 5 Hz raw reference ticks) both with `index_ids: ["BRTI"]`, not market tickers. Only indices, never personal portfolio, trades or order channels.

Every accepted observation has **three distinct clocks**: original CF Benchmarks source `source_ts_ms`, Kalshi-received `received_at`, and AXIOM's local wall-clock receive time. Source delay and local transit time are not conflated. Out-of-order, malformed, future-dated and nonfinite ticks fail closed. The provider's 60-second averaging window `window_size` distinguishes partial versus reported-complete; zero historical data or unauthenticated feeds are never retroactively labelled live. An available BRTI sample does not prove an available, executable prediction ahead of participant prices.

The `live_briti` API, `/health`, and iPhone-friendly dashboard are read-only. `BRTI_LIVE_ENABLED=1` and server-side secrets are mandatory to activate the worker; no key is bundled into the app. `DEMO_MODE=1` never opens a stream. If the stream is absent/late, its status is DISABLED/UNAVAILABLE/STALE. First STRIKE observation measures when AXIOM first received the current contract from public REST and **never asserts the exchange's first publication**. Existing Coinbase models do not vote with BRTI and all real order placement remains unavailable.
