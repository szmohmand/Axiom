AXIOM v1.0 is an original research implementation informed by published market-data conventions. It does not contain private code from any third-party trading bot. Its probabilities have not been fitted or calibrated against independent historical outcomes. Kalshi contract prices are quotes generated by participant orders, not Kalshi's model predictions. AXIOM uses them only for comparison and hypothetical execution; Coinbase spot is a proxy, not the official settlement benchmark. Paper positions assume instant execution at the displayed ask and exclude exchange fees, spread movement, funding, and slippage. No live orders or wallet access exist in this software.
